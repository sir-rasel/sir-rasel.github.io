Experienced in driving cross-functional engineering initiatives, mentoring engineers, and leading technical execution for business-critical products. Passionate about building scalable, maintainable, and resilient systems while aligning engineering decisions with business goals and customer needs.

- Readability and long-term maintainability

- Delivering business value through practical technical solutions
